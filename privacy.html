Privacy Policy

DVLIVING FOTURA Production Controller is a private production automation application.

Google Drive Access

The application accesses the authorized user's Google Drive to read production order files, upload generated print files and QC thumbnails, organize production history, verify uploaded files, and recover interrupted upload transactions.

Use of Google User Data

Google user data is used only to operate the production automation workflow. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Storage

Production files remain in the authorized Google Drive account and on the production computer where required by the workflow. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the production computer for authentication.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not transferred to unrelated third parties.

Access Revocation

The Google account owner may revoke this application's access through their Google Account security settings.
